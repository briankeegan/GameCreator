# Newsey — what the plot has that the game doesn't

Read `reference/the-game/PLOT.md` first, and the verbatim plot it distils
(the owner's long comment on Clubhouse PR #30) before acting on anything
here — the distilled file has lost detail that turned out to decide things.

This is a checklist of beats and systems the plot describes and the game
does not have yet, roughly biggest first. It is not a plan and nothing here
is agreed — it exists so the gap is written down instead of re-derived.

## Story beats

- **The man at your door.** Straight after the mirror's welcome: *"I was
  shaken out of my thoughts by a knock at the door… 'Nella. Open the door.'
  It was a male voice and it sounded like someone my age. The voice had a
  tone of authority… Instead of an answer, the door opened on its own. In my
  doorway stood a man in—"*. Not in the game at all: you wake, talk to the
  mirror, and the room is empty after that.

## Systems

- **The mirror as your screen.** The plot makes the giant mirror the
  menu/screen — it's how the devil appears in the first place. In the game
  it only carries the welcome and the practice duel. Rank, bracelet,
  playstyle gems, duel record: none of it is on there.
- **The clock.** *"A day in Infinity = a minute in the real world; you have
  ~1 hour (real) / ~59 'days' to escape or die."* Nothing tracks it. The
  save file counts playtime, but it isn't the plot's clock and nothing
  depends on it.
- **Bracelets as rank.** copper → silver → gold → diamond for rank, gem
  colour for playstyle. Characters describe their own bracelets in dialogue
  (Nella's copper/aquamarine, Timothy's all-diamond) but nothing is modelled
  — winning duels changes no rank.

## Places

Built: your old room, your father's house, your room in Infinity, the
Lounge, the Arena, the **Anarchy Garden**, **Kyran's Lab**, the Library.
Three doors are drawn but locked ("The word doesn't take your finger. Not
yet."): an Observatory, a Basement and an Office — none are in the plot by
those names, so what's behind them is open.

That covers every place the plot names.

## Characters

Present and speaking: Chuck, the devil/host, Kat, May 2000, Timothy,
Michael, John Boxley, Kyran.

Not in the game yet: **Diamond**, **Eric**, **Magma**, **Rex**, and
**Anarchy / Anthony** — the player who supposedly escaped, Michael's best
friend, namesake of the garden.

## Duels

Three exist: the host's practice bout, Kat, and May 2000. The plot's first
three levels are the campaign vs the angry cloud creature (which happens on
the cartridge at home, before Infinity — not built), the cursed unlock that
turns the board red, and Kat.
